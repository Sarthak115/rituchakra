# Nagar · Chapter 05 · The Long Journey (script draft for approval)

**Status:** approved by Sarthak Kumar on 2 Nov 2026 and built as `data/stories/theLongJourney.ts` (route `/nagar`).
**World:** Nagar (Modern Life) · **Setting:** a school trip by bus to the city science museum, then a roadside stop with a chemist shop.
**Learning objective:** a period can start away from home. Plan a little, ask for help in public without shame, and buy or share products as an everyday thing.
**Characters:** Aara (the one with the period) and Aarav (classmate, ally). The teacher and a friend are mentioned, not shown. Aarav never menstruates.
**Shape:** same as The Stain: Aara's moment → choice → consequence → switch to Aarav's view → choice → consequence → reflect → Science Check → resolution → reflection question.
**Background:** a new illustrated roadside stop: the parked school bus, a small chemist shop with a green cross, a tea stall, and the city skyline in the distance.

## Scene 3: Reflect

> Periods don't follow a timetable. A little planning and a lot of kindness go a long way.

## Science Check (every claim from the evidence register)

- **What we heard:** "If my period starts while travelling, I have to manage on my own."
- **What evidence says:** "Periods can vary and can start suddenly, especially in the first few years. Carrying a pad and a small bag for used products helps when you are away from home. Everyone needs a clean, private place to change. Used pads go in a bin, never down the toilet."
- **Remember:** "Plan a little. Ask for help. Never make it a secret or a joke."
- **Sources:**
  - UNFPA FAQ: periods "can also change or vary, particularly in the first few years".
  - UNICEF *Talking about periods at home*: carry products in your bag "if her period suddenly starts"; carry used reusables home "in a bag"; disposal "in a waste bin and never down flushing toilets".
  - WHO fact sheet: the need for "safe, clean and private rooms in which they can change".

No new health claims beyond these.
